# Artes — Golden Music Memories

Método: gerador de imagens integrado, sem uso de CLI. Fundo, capa e avatar foram gerados com IA. Não foram utilizadas fotos de artistas ou capas de álbuns. Os prompts abaixo permitem repetir a direção visual. A capa pronta é uma imagem plana; o modelo editável, quando incluído no pacote, é uma composição gráfica separada.

## Capa — prompt final aplicado ao fundo original

Edit this original cassette photograph into a finished premium TikTok cover for the music nostalgia channel Golden Music Memories. Preserve photorealistic cassette, black/petrol background and amber light aesthetic. Output portrait 9:16 cover ideally 1080x1920. Add very crisp professional editorial typography, all comfortably inside the central safe area horizontally (left 10% to right 82%) and vertically between 18% and 74% so interface controls and grid cropping do not hide it. Exact text top small but legible in warm gold: 'GOLDEN MUSIC' then 'MEMORIES'. Main headline enormous bold ivory clean condensed sans serif with refined spacing, two lines: 'TAKE' then 'ON ME'. Under main headline: 'a-ha' in medium-size warm gold sans serif. Nearby a small outlined label '1985'. No invented additional labels. Cassette should stay visible in the lower half, with slight crop if necessary, title and artist above it, no overlapping letterforms on bright cassette highlights. Very high-end music editorial, disciplined typography, premium restrained black, antique gold and cream. No artist portraits, album covers, fake artist signature, logos of other brands, no hearts, no slogan, no watermark. Exact spelling of all text mandatory. This is the channel's standard music-title cover; prioritize excellent small-size readability.

## Avatar — prompt final

Use case logo-brand. Create a finished square profile avatar for a premium music nostalgia channel named Golden Music Memories. A single beautiful original GMM monogram, all three capital letters very legible in a refined bold editorial serif, subtle warm metallic antique gold on near-black petrol background. Monogram inside a thin golden circular outline suggesting the concentric groove of a vinyl record, but never obscure lettering. Elegant flat graphic design with a very subtle fine grain, not a 3D mockup. One tiny abstract audio wave beneath GMM, integrated into balanced typography. Center everything with generous margins for circular cropping. Avatar must remain crisp and legible at 48 pixels. Exact and only text: 'GMM'. No other text, no artist photos, no cassette, no watermark, no existing brand imitation. Output square ideally1024x1024.

## Fundo — prompt final

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium cinematic background photograph for a new Brazilian music nostalgia channel covering the 1970s, 1980s, 1990s and 2000s. Vertical portrait 9:16 composition. A beautiful realistic translucent smoked-black compact audio cassette lying diagonally on a dark wood surface in the lower half of the image, lit by a narrow amber-orange beam from the left and a muted petrol teal rim light from the right. Detailed tape reels, fine brushed metal, tiny dust in light, warm authentic analog atmosphere, believable photographed object not an illustration. Background is deep nearly black petrol with subtle atmospheric film grain; generous calm dark negative space in the upper 55% for title typography to be added later. Restrained editorial music magazine art direction, tactile and elegant, not kitsch, not neon synthwave. Cassette occupies lower center, fits completely inside frame, not too small. No people, no artists, no band logos, no copyrighted album covers. Absolutely NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO NUMBERS, NO watermark or border. This is an original art asset, not a reproduction of any existing channel.
